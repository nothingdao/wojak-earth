# Earth 2089 Story Development Outline

## Game Overview
- **Setting**: Earth 2089, post-Fracture War world
- **Characters**: Male (redemption arc - Mad Max archetype) and Female (preservation arc - Snow White archetype)
- **Progression**: Level 1-33, chapter-based storytelling
- **Platform**: TypeScript React app with shared Convex backend

## World Building Elements
- **Core Themes**: Post-Fracture aftermath, Consor territories, augmented divide, resource scarcity, radio networks, the old coin network
- **Universal Threads**: The Restoration Project, Memory Wars, The Convergence, the chain
- **Regional Variations**: Neo-Singapore, Sahara Green, New Antarctica, The Rust Belt, Keirutsu Towers
- **Convergence Points**: The Blackout Event (L10-12), The Revelation (L20-22), The Choice (L30-33)

## Character Arcs
- **Male**: Fall from grace → Wandering → Reckoning → Return as protector
- **Female**: Protected innocent → Gathering storm → Testing → Inspiration/beacon

## Technical Approach
- Chapter-based database structure with story flags
- Iterative content development
- Decision consequences affecting health, skills, items, relationships
- Player progression tracking for future chapter prerequisites
- Solana blockchain integration for in-game economy and Earth currency trading
- Radio tower network mechanics for accessing the chain and long-distance communication

## Chapter Intersection Points
- **Chapters 10-11**: Both characters experience "The Signal" - same event, different perspectives
- **Chapters 20-21**: "Convergence" chapters - major world revelation affects both
- **Chapters 30-31**: "The Choice" - final decisions that shape Season 2
- **Potential Crossover**: Chapters 17-18 could feature direct character interaction

## Development Priority
1. **Phase 1**: Chapters 1-8 (establishing character voices and world)
2. **Phase 2**: Chapters 9-16 (expanding world, introducing major themes)
3. **Phase 3**: Chapters 17-24 (major conflicts and character development)
4. **Phase 4**: Chapters 25-33 (climax and Season 2 setup)
