## Story Flag System Integration

### Flag Categories

1. **Character Traits**: Core personality aspects (lone_wolf, hero_instinct, etc.)
2. **Faction Relations**: Standing with major groups (consor_contact, peaceful_contact, etc.)
3. **Skill Specializations**: Areas of expertise (tech_savvy, medical_skills, etc.)
4. **Moral Choices**: Ethical decisions (forgiver, avenger, etc.)
5. **World Knowledge**: Understanding of the setting (truth_seeker, mystery_solver, etc.)

### Flag Combinations

- **Leadership Path**: natural_leader + hero_instinct + alliance_leader
- **Rebel Path**: defiant_rebel + consor_hacked + truth_seeker
- **Survivor Path**: survival_instinct + lone_wolf + paranoid_survivor
- **Diplomat Path**: peaceful_contact + negotiator + alliance_builder

### Flag Progression

- **Chapters 1-8**: Basic character establishment (3-5 flags per character)
- **Chapters 9-16**: Faction and skill development (5-8 flags per character)
- **Chapters 17-24**: Moral and knowledge development (8-12 flags per character)
